# Safety, standards, and compliance

## Purpose

Safety prevents unacceptable harm to people, property, society, or the
environment. Standards and compliance encode some obligations, but passing a
checklist does not establish safety or ethical acceptability.

## Criteria

- Applicable language/framework conventions, style, and formatting.
- Accessibility and internationalization obligations.
- Safety, regulatory, legal, and contractual constraints.
- Hazard identification, risk controls, and safe-state behavior.
- Traceability from obligation to evidence and accountable approval.

## Review questions

- What harm can occur if the system is wrong, late, unavailable, or misused?
- Which laws, standards, policies, or accessibility criteria apply?
- Does failure move the system to a safe state, and can operators intervene?
- Is residual risk explicitly accepted by someone with authority?

## Warning signals and failure modes

Unclassified hazards, inaccessible interaction, locale-blind behavior,
untraceable requirements, bypassable controls, and compliance-by-assertion can
cause physical harm, exclusion, legal breach, unsafe operation, and lost trust.

## Strong evidence

Hazard analyses, accessibility testing with users and tools, compliance
matrices, safety cases, independent assessment, incident drills, and signed risk acceptance.
