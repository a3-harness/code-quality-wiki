# Wiki 02 demo: specialized research

## Demonstrated

- A 13-topic research plan with boundaries and source-selection rules.
- A machine-readable registry with unique source IDs and authority, currency,
  licensing, applicability, and local-copy metadata.
- Rights-safe snapshots of PEP 249, NIST SSDF 1.1, and NIST AI 600-1, verified
  by SHA-256.
- A published reference map, conflict/gap report, and acquisition list.
- A testable backlog for all 13 specialized pieces.

## Commands

```console
python scripts/check_references.py
python scripts/publish_root.py
bash /code/scripts/validate.sh
```
